#+title: Justice

The maintenance or administration of what is right in a fair and impartial way.

just or deserved

Jehovah show no partiality in dealing with his creatures.

Man is in no position to judge God's acts as just or unjust,
but must learn to conform his thinking to the standard of justice
that Jehovah has revealed in his word.

also, Jehovah's creatorship rules out all basis for questioning the rightness of his activities.

Jehovah has always rightly required that those desiring to gain his approval acquaint themselves with his standard of justice.

God has interwoven the laws of eternal justice with the happiness of each individual.
