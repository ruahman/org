#+title: appointment

Just as Jehovah uses holy spirit to appoint a man,
Jehovah will use the same holy spirit to remove a man from serving as an overseer
if he fails to manifest the fruitage of the spirit and his pattern of life fails short
of the Scriptureal requirements.
