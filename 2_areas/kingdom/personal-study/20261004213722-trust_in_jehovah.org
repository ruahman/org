the spiritual food we receive from the governing body. 
and our brother hood.

Because of these points just mentioned we can be confident that Jehovah will protect those who put his trust in him
both Spiritually, Emotionally, and Physically.
so we do not need to be afraid of the earthshaking events that will come.
