#+title: 1 Corinthians 3:18,19
#+filetags: :bible:let-him-become-a-fool:

If anyone among you thinks he is wise in this system of things,
let him become a fool, so that he may become wise.
for the wisdom of this world is foolishness with God,
for it is written: "He catches the wise in their own cunning"
