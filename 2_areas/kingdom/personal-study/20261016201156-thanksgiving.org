#+title: thanksgiving

expressing our gratitude to Jehovah in prayer, can help us to not feel anxious.

because when we express gratitude, we shift focus away from what we may or may not have tomorrow
to what Jehovah is doing for us now and what he has done for us.

we can reflect on how he has cared for us until now

we strengthen our confidence that he will continue to care for us in the future.
