#+title: 1-Timothy 3:1-7
#+filetags: :bible:reaching-out:fine-work:

If a man is reaching out to be an overseer,
he is desirous of a fine work.
the overseer should therefor be irreprehensible,
a husband of one wife,
moderate in habits,
sound in mind,
orderly,
hospitable,
qualified to teach,
not a drunkard,
not violent,
but reasonable,
not quarrelsome,
not a lover of money,
a man presiding over his own household in a fine manner,
having his children in subjection with all sesriousness
for if a man does not know how to preside over his own household,
how will he care for the congregation of God?
not a newly converted man, for fear that he might ge puffed up with pride
and fall into the judgment passed on the Devil.
Moreover, he should also have a fine testimony from outsiders so that he does not fall into reproach
and a snare of the Devil.
