#+title: Matthew 28:19,20
#+filetags: :bible:make-disciples:

Go, therefore, and make disciples of peoples of all the nations,
baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son
and of the holy spirit,
teaching them to observe all the things I have commanded you.
And look! I am with you all the days until the conclusion of the system of things.
