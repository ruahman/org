* Comprehensive Inline LaTeX Examples

** Basic math delimiters

Dollar signs (most common): $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$

Single-character shortcut (no spaces needed): $x$ is a variable, $n$ items

Escaped parens for inline math: \(f(x) = x^2 + 2x + 1\)

Escaped brackets for displayed/block math:
\[
\lim_{x \to \infty} \frac{1}{x} = 0
\]

** Subscripts, superscripts, and fractions

Subscript: $a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_n$

Superscript with braces for multi-character: $2^{10} = 1024$

Nested fractions: $\cfrac{1}{1 + \cfrac{1}{1 + \cfrac{1}{2}}}$

Simple fraction: $\frac{d}{dx}\left(x^2\right) = 2x$

** Greek letters and symbols

Common Greek letters: $\alpha, \beta, \gamma, \delta, \pi, \theta, \lambda, \sigma$

Special symbols: $\infty, \partial, \nabla, \forall, \exists, \emptyset$

Set notation: $A \subseteq B, \quad x \in \mathbb{R}, \quad \emptyset \subset A$

** Sums, integrals, products

Summation: $\sum_{i=1}^{n} i = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}$

Integral with bounds: $\int_{0}^{1} x^2 \, dx = \frac{1}{3}$

Product notation: $\prod_{k=1}^{n} k = n!$

Double integral: $\iint_D f(x,y) \, dA$

** Matrices and vectors

Inline vector: $\vec{v} = (v_1, v_2, v_3)$

Matrix (displayed):
\[
M = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix}
\]

Determinant notation:
\[
\det(A) = \begin{vmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{vmatrix} = ad - bc
\]

** Multi-line aligned equations

\begin{align}
f(x) &= (x+1)^2 \\
     &= x^2 + 2x + 1
\end{align}

** Named equation environments (numbered)

\begin{equation}
E = mc^2
\end{equation}

\begin{equation}
\label{eq:euler}
e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0
\end{equation}

You can reference it later: as shown in Equation \ref{eq:euler}.

** Text mixed with math inline

If $n$ is even, then $n^2$ is also even, since $n = 2k$ implies
$n^2 = 4k^2 = 2(2k^2)$.

** Special LaTeX commands Org handles

Bold math: $\mathbf{v} \cdot \mathbf{w}$

Blackboard bold (common for number sets): $\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{Z}, \mathbb{Q}, \mathbb{R}, \mathbb{C}$

Text inside math mode: $f(x) = x^2 \text{ for all } x \in \mathbb{R}$
